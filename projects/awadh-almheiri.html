Awadh Almheiri Law Firm

The tricky part Publish once, appear in six languages: every post is translated in the background, so the team never waits and never needs a developer.

Six-language website for a Dubai law firm with AI translation on publish, SEO-friendly localised URLs and an admin panel the team runs on its own.

Visit live site

Overview

Awadh Almheiri Law Firm and Legal Consultancy is a Dubai law firm serving individuals, corporations and government entities. The firm needed a website that could speak to clients in many languages, rank well in search and be managed entirely by its own team.

My role

Full-stack developer on the website and its content management: architecture, the multilingual system, SEO and the admin experience.

What I built

A complete firm website

  • Home with an animated hero, a services grid, practice highlights, the sectors the firm serves and scrolling strips of clients and partners.
  • About the office: the firm’s profile, a message from the managing partner, identity, mission and vision, approach and team.
  • Services organised by audience (individuals, corporations, government entities) and sector pages such as construction, healthcare and energy.
  • Global cooperation: an interactive 3D globe that highlights every country the firm works with.
  • News and blog with live search, category filters and related posts.
  • A consultation request form so prospective clients can reach the right office easily.

Built for every language and for search

  • The whole site in six languages: Arabic, English, French, Russian, Chinese and Urdu.
  • AI-powered translation, so new articles appear in every language as soon as they are published.
  • Clean, readable, language-specific URLs, an automatic multilingual sitemap, breadcrumbs, and redirects that kept older links working.

Easy to run

  • A friendly admin panel for articles, categories and site content, with a rich editor and scheduled publishing.
  • A built-in email newsletter tool for the firm’s own communications.

Technical highlights

  • Laravel 12 with Tailwind CSS, Alpine.js and MySQL.
  • Background jobs for translation and email so publishing always feels instant.
  • An SEO structure where every page has one canonical address in each language.

Outcome

The firm runs a six-language website that keeps itself translated, is easy to find in search, and gives its team full control over what they publish.
