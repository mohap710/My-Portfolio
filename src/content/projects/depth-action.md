## Overview

Depth Action Organization (DAO) is a humanitarian NGO working across Sudan in education, child protection, food security, shelter, health and peacebuilding. Its previous website was hard to update and did not reflect the scale of the organisation's work.

I designed and built a new bilingual website with an easy content management panel, and carried all of the existing content across to it.

## My role

End to end: shaping the scope with the organisation, exploring design directions, the full build, the content migration and the launch.

## What I built

### A design the team chose

- Three complete design directions, **Architectural Light**, **Bento Dashboard** and **Field Stories**, each previewed on desktop, tablet and phone so the team could compare them and pick one with confidence.

### A bilingual public website

- Real **Arabic and English** content with a language switch on every page and a full right-to-left layout in Arabic.
- **Home**: rotating hero messages, the organisation's mission, its key sectors, headline impact figures ("Measured in lives, not line items"), latest news, partners and team.
- **Programmes**: the sectors the organisation works in, from education and protection to women's empowerment and good governance.
- **Projects**: an overview of current and past work with a dedicated page for each project.
- **Where we work**, **partners**, **donate**, **volunteer**, **careers**, **news** and **contact** pages, so every audience has a clear path.

### Content management

- An admin panel that lets the team update news, projects, programmes, impact figures, partners, team and vacancies without a developer, writing both languages side by side.

## Technical highlights

- **Laravel** with **Tailwind CSS** and **Alpine.js**, built mobile-first and accessible.
- A bilingual content model, so every page is complete in both languages.
- Migrated the previous site's news and project content into the new structure, cleaned of old page-builder markup.

## Outcome

The organisation now presents itself to donors and partners with a modern, trustworthy site in both languages, and its own team keeps the content fresh.
