## Overview

Afro-Tech is a professional training centre in Sudan offering a wide range of courses to the local community, and one of the country's most trusted training providers.

## My role

Back-end developer: the server-side application and data layer behind the centre's website.

## What I built

- A **Node.js and Express back-end** serving the website's content and course information.
- A **MySQL** database for courses and related records.
- The endpoints the front-end uses to list and present the centre's training programmes.

## Technical highlights

- A clean Express structure with separated routes, controllers and data access, easy to extend as the course catalogue grows.

## Outcome

A reliable back-end that lets the centre present its courses online and keep them up to date.
