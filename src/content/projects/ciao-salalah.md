## Overview

Ciao Salalah is a tour operator inviting travellers to discover Salalah, Oman's emerald oasis. The site had to speak to two audiences, so it was built in **English and Italian**.

## My role

Frontend developer: design implementation and the bilingual build.

## What I built

- A bilingual **English / Italian** website with a language switch.
- Pages presenting tours, destinations and the operator's services, with photography that sells the destination.
- Responsive layouts for travellers browsing on phones as much as on desktop.

## Technical highlights

- Built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, keeping the site light and fast to load on mobile networks.

## Outcome

A welcoming bilingual site that introduces Salalah to English- and Italian-speaking travellers and leads them to book.
