## Overview

PixaTech is a technology company in the UAE. The brief was to take its Figma designs and turn them into a website that looks exactly like the design, on every screen size.

## My role

Frontend developer: the full build, from the Figma files to a live production website.

## What I built

- A **pixel-perfect implementation** of the Figma designs in semantic HTML, CSS and JavaScript: spacing, type sizes, colours and alignment matched to the design.
- Responsive layouts for desktop, tablet and mobile, including the service and solution showcases, so the design holds together at every width.
- Interactive details and transitions that follow the design's intent without weighing the page down.

## Technical highlights

- Careful translation of a static design into flexible, responsive CSS, deciding how each section should adapt between the designed breakpoints.
- Lean, hand-written front-end code with optimised assets and minimal JavaScript.

## Outcome

A website that is faithful to the brand's designs down to the detail, giving visitors a polished first impression of the company on any device.
