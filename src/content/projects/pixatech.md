## Overview

PixaTech is a technology company in the UAE. The brief was to turn its Figma designs into a fast, faithful website that works across every screen size.

## My role

Frontend developer: the full build from design files to a production website.

## What I built

- A **pixel-perfect implementation** of the Figma designs in semantic HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
- Responsive layouts tuned for desktop, tablet and mobile, including the service and solution showcases.
- Interactive details and transitions that follow the design without weighing the page down.

## Technical highlights

- Performance-first front-end practices: optimised assets, lean CSS and minimal JavaScript.
- **15% faster page loads** after optimisation, measured across devices.

## Outcome

A website that matches the brand's designs closely and loads quickly, giving visitors a smoother first impression of the company.
